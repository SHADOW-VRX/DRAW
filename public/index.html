How pairing works 
Codes are generated by the server using a cryptographically
secure random source, so they cannot be guessed.

Sessions expire automatically after a short idle period. No
artwork, video, or personal data is stored on the server —
only the pairing handshake and WebRTC signaling messages are
relayed between the two devices.

All media flows peer-to-peer over WebRTC. A STUN server helps
devices discover each other; on strict networks a TURN relay
may be required.

From screen to wall.

DRAW composites your camera, artwork and guides into a single real-time video stream that Device B displays.

  1. Enable your rear camera on HTTPS or localhost.
  2. Upload artwork and enter measured wall dimensions.
  3. Calibrate: drag all four handles to your wall corners.
  4. Lock the corners and keep the camera stationary.
  5. Create a session and note the code.
  6. Pair Device B, then start the live view.

Calibration is a manual perspective transform, not spatial AR tracking. Locking fixes the artwork coordinates; it does not freeze the camera.
